Drop your hero background photo here as:

    hero-bg.jpg

Recommended: a wide landscape/nature shot (rolling hills, forest canopy,
sunlight through leaves — anything that reads "sustainability"), at least
1600px wide. JPG or WebP both work — just keep the filename `hero-bg.jpg`,
or update the path in `src/components/landing/HeroPhoto.tsx` if you use a
different name or format.

Free, license-friendly sources: unsplash.com, pexels.com, pixabay.com.

If no file is present, the hero just falls back to the existing green
gradient/hills backdrop — nothing breaks.
